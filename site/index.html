the team runs itself

      
hand it something huge
give main a big goal. it splits it into jobs, runs the agents, restarts the ones that stop half-way, and keeps going until it's done.

      
worktrees? don't think about it
agents share your folder. when one needs its own copy, it makes a worktree, works there and cleans it up after. no branch to name, no folder to delete, nothing to keep in your head.

      
your head stays yours

      
main is always there
main gives the heavy work to the agents, so it's never busy. ask it anything, add one more thing, change your mind: it answers right away, and no job stops.

      
zen mode while you type
start typing and everything else fades: the agents, the counts, the chatter. just you and your words. send it and it all comes back. what the agents said to each other stays folded, ctrl+o opens it.

      
see what they made

      
everything they made, in one list
pages, docs, sheets, slides, PRs: what agents make for you lands in /artifacts. search it, open it, flip through its versions. in a reply, it's a link that opens it.

      
the diff, right there
click ± 3 files or press ctrl+g: an agent's changes against main open beside the thread, file by file. ⏎ opens the line in your editor.

      
hands off the keyboard

      
talk to it. it talks back
press ctrl+r twice and just talk to main. it answers out loud while the agents keep working, and blows you a kiss when it's done. /voice picks the model, the voice and the language.

      
it can use your browser
agents open pages in their own tab group, in the background, with your logins: they read, click, fill forms and take screenshots. never your screen, never your tab. Mac apps too. off until /computer-use.

      
no more "yes, continue"

      
you're not the router
main answers the agents for you: the obvious questions, the way you would, and it tells you why. only the real decisions reach you.

      
agents sync on their own
every agent can talk to every other one. they ask, share and hand off quietly, folded out of your way.

      
every tool you have

      
all your MCPs. all your skills. always on
GitHub, Linear, Sentry, Slack, your docs, your database: connect as many MCP servers as you want, and never pick which ones to turn on. bise calls tools through code, so a hundred servers don't fill its context.

      
bring your Agent Plugins
skills, MCP servers, hooks: plugins in the Agent Plugins format load as they are, from ~/.agents/plugins or your repo. Vibe plugins too.

      
you're still the boss

      
talk to any agent, anytime
⌥ + a number, or @name. ask it why, push it, then go back to main. nobody has to stop.

      
fits your PR flow
in a repo that takes pull requests, every change gets its own branch and its own PR, then goes through your usual flow: CI, review bots, teammates. the panel shows where each one stands.

questions

    
but i already have Claude Code / Codex / Vibe
keep your models, MCP servers and skills. bise changes how you work with them: you talk to a team lead instead of juggling sessions.

    
won't the agents step on each other?
they tell each other what they touch before they touch it. worktrees only when one needs its own copy, cleaned up after.

    
does it ask before running a command?
by default, no: that's yolo, everything runs. shift+tab switches to auto: safe commands just run, risky ones ask you first in your inbox, and it all runs in a macOS sandbox.

    
will a single thread scale to a project as complex as mine?
yes. main doesn't carry your code: the agents do the reading, each one with a fresh context. main keeps the plan and the reports, so the thread stays light. when it gets long, it compacts and keeps going. tested on a repo with millions of lines of code.

    
won't my context overflow? i don't want to live in the dumb zone
no need to overthink it. when a thread gets long, bise compacts it. the latest models are trained for that and they keep their trajectory. your thread just keeps going.

    
do i need a memory system?
nope. compaction carries the thread, and any agent can go back and read the full history: every message, every tool call. nothing gets lost.

    
how many agents can i run?
as many as you want. the real limits are your tokens, your budget and your provider.

    
what does it run on?
macOS for now, Apple silicon and Intel. linux is next. windows later, maybe through WSL first. you need git, and that's about it.

    
which models?
bring your own key: Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral and more. the first run lists every provider.

    
do i need to learn anything?
four words: you, main, agents, inbox. main is the one you talk to. the rest you pick up in the first hour.

    
open source?
yes, under Apache-2.0. read it, fork it, send a :*.
