the team plan's price

one decision before the pricing page ships

what does the team plan cost per seat?

  1. €12, the same as today
  2. €15, with SSO included
  3. €10 yearly, €12 monthly

at €15, the 40 teams on the plan pay €3,600 more a month; 9 of them asked for SSO in the last quarter1. the yearly discount brought 30% of teams to yearly at Linear's size2, but we have no number of our own.

  • €12: nothing to explain, SSO stays a paid add-on.
  • €15: one price, one email to send, a few churn risks.
  • yearly: cash up front, the billing code needs a week.

a risk

two teams wrote they would leave over a price rise. both are on the old €9 plan, which stays.
